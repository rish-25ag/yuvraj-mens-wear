The Story of Yuvraj

About us · since 2002

Ravi Agarwal opened Yuvraj Mens Wear in 2002 on Shiv Thakur Lane, in Daccapatty, Burrabazar.

For more than 20 years he has helped Kolkata's men find the right clothes, from a first office shirt to a kurta for a family wedding. He picks the stock himself, keeps Burrabazar prices, and makes sure there is a size for everyone, all the way up to 5XL.

Come to the shop and ask for Ravi ji. He'll help you put the whole outfit together.
